## Shared Prompt Direction

Portrait 4:5; one identifiable component, fully visible and centered with ample
margins; dark steel-grey textured tabletop; neutral soft industrial light;
realistic materials; no vehicle scenery, text overlays, logos, certification
marks or collage. Style reference: existing `railway-axle-box.webp`.
