# Gangway and bellows illustrations

These five WebP assets were created with the built-in image generation tool.
They are illustrative product visualizations, not photographs of Railwheel
inventory or approved engineering designs. Cards and detail pages disclose this.
Original generated PNG files remain in the Codex generated-images directory.
WebP exports are 800px wide at quality 82; no existing product photos were replaced.

## Prompt Set

Common direction: product-mockup; one photorealistic industrial catalog product,
three-quarter view, entire assembly centered with generous margins, seamless pale
grey studio backdrop, soft neutral lighting, realistic materials, no text, logos,
watermarks, people, vehicles or collage.

- `railway-gangway-illustration.webp`: complete passenger gangway assembly with
  rectangular silver mounting frames, black accordion bellows and metal walking floor.
- `railway-gangway-bellows-illustration.webp`: bellows-only flexible rectangular
  enclosure, coated-fabric convolutions and mounting lips, without a floor bridge.
- `inter-car-connection-illustration.webp`: silver passenger passage frames,
  overlapping stainless floor bridge plates and short black flexible side enclosure.
- `bus-articulated-bellows-illustration.webp`: tall wide bus articulation bellows
  shell, grey coated-fabric folds, rounded upper corners and slim attachment frames.
- `flexible-vehicle-bellows-illustration.webp`: short rectangular black elastomer
  transition boot, four deep convolutions, rounded corners and mounting flanges,
  without a walking floor.

Replace these files with approved model-specific photography when available and
update the visible disclosures, alt text and structured data accordingly.
